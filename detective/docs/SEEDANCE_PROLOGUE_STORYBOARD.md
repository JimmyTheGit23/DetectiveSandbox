# 序章「渡口沉舟」Seedance 片头短剧分镜

> **时长目标**：90–120 秒（8–10 个镜头）  
> **风格基调**：明代万历年间 · 冬雨夜 · 水墨质感 · 暗调悬疑  
> **画面比例**：16:9  
> **整体氛围**：冷色调为主，仅火光/灯笼为暖色点缀

---

## 分镜 1 — 暴雨中的夜船（建立镜头）

**时长**：8–10 秒  
**镜头**：大远景 → 缓慢推近  

**English Prompt:**
```
Cinematic wide shot, a small wooden sailboat gliding through a dark river at night during heavy rain in ancient China, Ming Dynasty era. The boat is illuminated by a single lantern swinging in the wind. Torrential rain creates silver streaks against the black water surface. Dense fog rolls over the riverbanks. Dark blue and charcoal color palette. The camera slowly pushes forward toward the boat. Atmospheric, moody, ink-wash painting aesthetic. 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

**中文参考**：暴雨夜，一艘木帆船在漆黑的江面上行驶。唯一的灯笼在风雨中摇晃。大雨如帘，江面漆黑。远处雾气笼罩两岸。

---

## 分镜 2 — 船底进水（危机开始）

**时长**：6–8 秒  
**镜头**：低角度 → 船底特写  

**English Prompt:**
```
Low angle shot inside a wooden ship cabin, Ming Dynasty China. Dark water rapidly gushing through a perfectly square hole carved in the wooden hull from below. The water rushes in violently, flooding the cabin floor. A young man in sleeping clothes wakes up in shock, sitting up on a wooden bunk. His face shows alarm as cold water reaches his ankles. Dim amber lantern light from above. Camera shakes slightly to convey urgency. Dark moody atmosphere. 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

**中文参考**：船底一个方形洞口，冰水从中涌出，迅速淹没船舱地板。一个年轻男子从睡梦中惊醒，水已没过脚踝。昏暗的灯光摇曳。

---

## 分镜 3 — 逃生（动作高潮）

**时长**：8–10 秒  
**镜头**：手持跟拍 → 仰角  

**English Prompt:**
```
Dramatic action shot inside a sinking wooden ship cabin, Ming Dynasty China. A young man stands on a wooden crate, water up to his chest, desperately prying open a ceiling hatch with an iron crowbar. Water splashes violently. He breaks through the lock, rain pours in from above. He pulls himself up through the hatch with bleeding hands. Camera follows from below looking up. Blue-black water, harsh rain light from above. Intense, desperate energy. 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

**中文参考**：水已到胸口的船舱中，年轻男子踩在木箱上，用铁撬棍撬开天窗。锁扣断裂，雨水灌入。他双手带血地爬出天窗。

---

## 分镜 4 — 落水与沉船（命运转折）

**时长**：6–8 秒  
**镜头**：水下 → 水面仰拍  

**English Prompt:**
```
Split shot: above water showing a wooden ship breaking apart and sinking in a dark rainstorm river at night, and below water showing a young man sinking into black water, arms reaching upward toward the surface light. Debris and wooden planks float around him. His robes billow in the current. A leather document pouch detaches from his waist and drifts away into darkness. Ethereal, tragic beauty. Dark blue and silver tones. 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

**中文参考**：船体断裂沉入江中。水下视角：年轻男子在黑暗中下沉，手臂伸向水面的微光。腰间的公文袋脱落，漂入黑暗。木板碎片四散。

---

## 分镜 5 — 获救（希望之光）

**时长**：8–10 秒  
**镜头**：中景 → 特写  

**English Prompt:**
```
A young woman in dark blue martial arts attire with a messenger satchel drags an unconscious young man onto a muddy riverbank at dawn. She kneels over him, pressing his back to expel water, her hair plastered to her face by rain. Desperate determination in her eyes. The man coughs violently and opens his eyes. First grey morning light breaks through storm clouds. Wet sand, scattered reeds. Emotional, hopeful despite the bleak setting. 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

**中文参考**：黎明时分，一个穿暗蓝劲装的年轻女子将昏迷的男子拖上河岸。她跪在他身旁按压后背排水。男子剧烈咳嗽，睁开眼睛。灰色晨光穿透乌云。

---

## 分镜 6 — 码头尸体（案件开端）

**时长**：6–8 秒  
**镜头**：俯拍 → 推近  

**English Prompt:**
```
Aerial shot slowly pushing down toward a muddy riverside dock at dawn, Ming Dynasty China. A body lies face-down half submerged in shallow muddy water. A crowd of villagers in traditional winter robes forms a circle around it, pointing and murmuring. Rain has eased to a drizzle. Grey overcast sky. A young man in simple clothes approaches the body and kneels beside it. The atmosphere is heavy with dread. Muted earth tones. 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

**中文参考**：俯拍渡口码头，一具尸体面朝下泡在浅水中。围观村民形成一圈。雨已变小。一个年轻男子走向尸体蹲下查看。灰暗阴沉的天色。

---

## 分镜 7 — 指控（冲突爆发）

**时长**：8–10 秒  
**镜头**：正反打 → 特写  

**English Prompt:**
```
Close-up shot of a grief-stricken middle-aged woman in white mourning clothes lunging forward, grabbing the collar of a young man's robe. Her face is twisted with rage and tears. She screams accusations while the young man remains calm but tense. Behind them, villagers watch with mixed expressions of suspicion and sympathy. A portly middle-aged man in official robes steps forward to intervene. Rain continues. Tense, confrontational energy. Traditional Ming Dynasty setting. 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

**中文参考**：穿白色丧服的中年妇女扑向年轻男子，抓住他的衣领，满脸悲愤。男子保持冷静但紧绷。身后村民表情各异。一个穿官服的圆脸中年人上前调解。

---

## 分镜 8 — 决心（转折宣言）

**时长**：6–8 秒  
**镜头**：中景 → 慢推特写  

**English Prompt:**
```
Medium shot of a young man standing in the rain at a riverside dock, Ming Dynasty China. His robes are torn and damp, but his posture is upright and resolute. He looks directly at the camera with cold, determined eyes. Behind him, a young woman in blue stands beside him, equally determined. The grey river and distant mountains form the backdrop. Rain falls around them but they stand still. A moment of quiet defiance. Cinematic, powerful. 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

**中文参考**：雨中码头，年轻男子衣衫破损但站姿挺拔，目光冷峻坚定地直视前方。身后穿蓝衣的年轻女子同样坚定地站在身旁。灰色江面和远山为背景。

---

## 分镜 9 — 标题卡（收束）

**时长**：4–6 秒  
**镜头**：静态 → 渐显  

**English Prompt:**
```
Black screen fading in to reveal a rain-soaked ancient Chinese ferry crossing at dusk, seen through falling rain like a Chinese ink-wash painting. The image slowly fades as elegant Chinese calligraphy text appears in the center: "渡口沉舟". Subtitle below: "The Ferry Sinking". Rain continues to fall across the screen. Dark atmospheric, cinematic title card. 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

**中文参考**：全黑画面渐显，雨中的古代渡口如水墨画般呈现。画面渐隐，中央浮现书法标题「渡口沉舟」，下方副标题 "The Ferry Sinking"。

## 技术备注

| 项目 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **风格关键词** | `cinematic, ink-wash aesthetic, Ming Dynasty, dark blue tones, atmospheric rain, moody lighting` |
| **角色一致性** | 每个 prompt 末尾追加角色描述锚点，确保同一角色跨镜头一致 |
| **陆昭** | `young Chinese man, mid-20s, sharp features, scholarly but athletic build, dark robes` |
| **凌瑶** | `young Chinese woman, early 20s, short messy hair, dark blue martial attire, messenger satchel, determined expression` |
| **周氏** | `middle-aged Chinese woman, white mourning clothes, grief-stricken face` |
| **镜头运动** | Seedance 对 `slowly pushes forward / camera follows / aerial shot` 响应较好 |
| **光线** | 统一使用 `dim lantern light / grey dawn / overcast` 保持视觉连贯 |
| **色调** | 全片统一 `dark blue, charcoal, muted earth tones`，仅灯笼/火光为暖色 |
| **帧率** | 建议 24fps，后期可做变速处理营造紧张感 |
